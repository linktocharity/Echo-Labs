The Ritual

Three steps to a living archive.

01

Upload & Sculpt

Open the EchoFrame app. Choose a photo — a wedding, a first word, a stadium, a kitchen table. Find the video from that day. Drag the 30-second window over the audio that holds the feeling. Every adjustment is felt, not just heard.

Via iPhone · Android
02

We Print & Encode

Your image is output on museum-grade baryta paper — the same stock archivists use for prints meant to outlast buildings. An NFC chip, invisible to the eye and impervious to time, is embedded into the substrate. Your audio is encoded and encrypted to the chip's unique identifier.

5–7 Day Fulfillment
03

Drop & Listen

Set the Hub on a shelf, a bedside table, a mantle. When a print is slipped into the slot, the Hub wakes — imperceptibly, deliberately — and the room fills with your memory. No screens. No unlocking. Just a photograph, a gesture, and 30 seconds of exactly then.

The EchoFrame App

The editor that thinks in feelings, not frames.

Most audio tools were built for podcasters. EchoFrame was built for the 30 seconds after your name was called, right before the vows, or the moment a three-year-old finally said "mama." The Reel-style slider makes finding that moment intuitive — because you already know exactly where it lives.

⧖

Reel-Style Window Editor

Scrub through any video. The 30-second window snaps, drags, and previews instantly. Trim with a single finger. What you hear is what gets encoded.

⬇

Video-to-Audio Extraction

Upload any video — 4K, phone footage, old MiniDV conversions. EchoFrame strips the audio, normalises levels, and gives you a clean waveform to work with. No format conversions needed.

⊕

Offline-First Architecture

Once ordered and synced, your audio lives on the Hub — not in a cloud that can be discontinued. Your memories are not a subscription.

Why EchoFrame

Photographs were never silent.

"Every image that has ever moved you did so because of what surrounded it — laughter in the next room, a song half-heard through an open window, the particular silence after something was said for the first time."

We built EchoFrame because the most important moments of a life are multi-sensory — and photography, as stunning as it has become, only ever captured half. The Hub and Echo-Print are a system, not a product. A ritual, not a gadget. You drop the print, and for 30 seconds, the room changes.

We use the same archival papers as the world's leading photographic galleries. Our NFC chips are rated for 100,000 read cycles. The Hub is machined from solid hardwood and natural stone. Nothing about this was built to be replaced.
